OFFSHORE RENEWABLE ENERGY

Advocates for co-ordinated and timely development of Ireland's huge offshore renewable energy (ORE) potential.

Ireland has a maritime area seven times its land area with some of the best wind/wave resources globally.

The Irish government has a target of developing ORE generation capacity ;

  • 5GW by 2030,
  • 20GW by 2040 and;
  • 37GW by 2050

Ireland's south coast has the potential for 3.5GW to 18GW of fixed-bottom wind

Ireland's Atlantic coast has the potential for 30GW of floating wind.
