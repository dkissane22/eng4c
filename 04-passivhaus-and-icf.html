ICF and the housing crisis in Ireland and throughout Europe

We should look at solutions from a previous housing crisis?

Insulated Concrete Formwork (ICF) construction - one such solution?

Use of lightweight expanded polystyrene (EPS) blocks as a form ( i.e. mould) was developed as a method to build much needed strong , high performance fast to construct , cost effective housing in post WW2 Europe.

Use of ICF can reduce the embodied carbon in a building by over 50%

see link

https://www.engineersireland.ie/Engineers-Journal/More/Sponsored/altherm-icf-reduces-embodied-carbon-by-50

ICF Design and achieving the Passivhaus standard

One of the main advantages of using a build method like ICF is through its high levels of thermal performance abd airtigtness.

The central concrete core sits between the insulating block formwork creating an airtight structure with high thermal performance

This results in a finished building with U-values as low as 0.10 leading to represents a saving of around 70% on heating costs when compared to standard constrcution techniques

An ICF building when properly designed and constructed easily meets the Passivhaus standard.
