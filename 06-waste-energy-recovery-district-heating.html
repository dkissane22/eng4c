WASTE ENERGY RECOVERY AND DISTRICT HEATING

Advocates for renewable heat and waste heat recovery. We are particularly focussed on thermal source networks ( 5th generation heat networks) using waste streams form industry including data-centres.

Data-centre waste heat recovery involves;

  • Collecting low grade waste heat from a data centre servers typically at 25°C.
  • Upgrading this to 75°C using an ammonia based industrial heat pump
  • Distributing heat at 65°C to heat homes and industry, returning at 55°C via a heat network.
  • Distributing cooled water back to the data-centre at 10°C to cool their servers.

Thus creating a highly energy efficient circular energy system
