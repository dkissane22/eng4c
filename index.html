What are the Sustainable Development Goals?

"A shared blueprint for peace and prosperity for people and the planet, now and into the future.”

Engineers for Climate Action focus on SDGs 7 and 13 as two complimentary SDG's promoting a sustainable energy sector .

What is Goal 7 - Affordable and Clean Energy

Goal 7 - is to ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern energy for all.

What is Goal 13 - Climate Action

Goal 13 - is to ensure effective Climate Action before irreversible damage is caused to our planet.
